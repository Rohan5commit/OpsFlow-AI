# Submission Description

OpsFlow AI is a high-impact internal operations request-routing and approval platform designed to solve the perennial "triage bottleneck." By integrating **NVIDIA NIM** (LLaMA 3.3 70B) for intelligent request triage, OpsFlow AI automates request classification, urgency detection, and deterministic routing, effectively reducing manual triage effort by ~65%.

### **Key Metrics & ROI**
- **Time Saved:** ~53 hours/month (based on 400 requests/month).
- **Automation:** Reduces manual handoffs by ~42%.
- **Intelligent Routing:** AI-driven triage ensures requests reach the correct approver (IT, Finance, or People Ops) with all data collected upfront, eliminating back-and-forth communication.

OpsFlow AI transforms operations from a reactive cost center into an efficient, AI-assisted productivity driver.
